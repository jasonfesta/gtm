# Email guidance

Read [copy.md](../copy.md), [the brief](../brief.md) and the approved email examples before drafting. Current guide finalized from explicit direction; no example-derived calibration is claimed.

Use an appropriate subject and an original note grounded in the actual recipient context. No mandatory greeting, research recap or closing question; combine the required introduction/offer/link naturally. A/B labels do not assign different rhetorical jobs.

The current storage limits are 60 body words and a 1–6 word subject. Lowercase prose; preserve exact identifiers. Keep factual claims within the brief. Save new local revisions; preserve existing Gmail and CRM drafts.

Initial introductions briefly identify the sender, what we are building and one concrete verified offer, ending with one exact verified link. Follow copy.md for developer-only fit, ordinary punctuation, short length and critique/rewrite checks. This defines content, not fixed sentence order. Missing links or unsupported claims are holds.
