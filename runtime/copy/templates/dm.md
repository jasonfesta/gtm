# DM guidance

Read [copy.md](../copy.md), [the brief](../brief.md) and approved examples for the exact platform. Current guide finalized from explicit direction; no example-derived calibration is claimed.

Treat LinkedIn and X DMs separately. Match the actual relationship and reason to write, without invented familiarity or a scripted opener or closing question; make the introduction and offer direct. A/B are editorial alternatives, not fixed scripts.

Current storage limit: 40 words, lowercase prose, exact identifiers preserved. Inspect channel access and history before preparation. Incoming replies require the actual incoming message; see [private replies](reply_dm.md).

Initial introductions briefly identify the sender, what we are building and one concrete verified offer, ending with one exact verified link. Follow copy.md for developer-only fit, ordinary punctuation, short length and critique/rewrite checks. This defines content, not fixed sentence order. Missing links or unsupported claims are holds.
