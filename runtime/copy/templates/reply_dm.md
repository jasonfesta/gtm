# Reply to an incoming DM

Read [copy.md](../copy.md), the brief and approved examples for the exact platform. Read the actual incoming message and relevant history.

Answer what the person actually said. A follow-up question or next step is optional, not a required second sentence. Respect a decline and do not invent promises, availability or capabilities.

Current storage limit: 35 words and lowercase prose; preserve exact identifiers. A/B are editorial alternatives without a prescribed structure.
