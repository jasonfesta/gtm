# Copy preparation workflow

Read [copy.md](copy.md), [brief](brief.md), [formats](three-flow-copy.md) and [contact policy](../Rules.md). The selected agent runbook defines the workload and provider workflow. Copy preparation creates no schedule and sends no message.

1. Use a source-backed identity and relevant current conversation. Verify the route, account, applicable suppressions and prior contact history.
2. Prepare concise contextual copy under the saved voice rules. Preserve exact addresses, handles and source URLs. Email receives final supplied copy and does not rewrite it.
3. Store drafts and revisions privately with source evidence and a stable action identity. Prepared, reserved, submitted, confirmed and uncertain states remain distinct.
4. Recheck eligibility and context before a requested action. An unanswered cold sequence uses each eligible channel at most once, at least 72 hours between actual sends and at most three touches. Engagement stops that sequence. These limits do not establish a minimum workload or authorize sending.
5. Record exact provider readback. Reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying, and pass separate CRM and body-free analytics handoffs to Ops.

Use [UTM formation](../UTM/FORMATION_RULES.md) for tracked links. Preserve registered campaign identifiers and exact saved destinations on retries. Keep recipient identities, daily batch IDs and message bodies out of URL tracking values.
