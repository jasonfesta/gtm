# Three copy formats

[copy.md](copy.md) governs voice through the real-example session; the [brief](brief.md) defines audience and offer. Channel formats are delivery categories, not fixed message structures. The [routing criteria](../../docs/OUTREACH_ROUTES.md) determine who gets email, social → email, contact outreach or location outreach. Do not confuse route numbers with writing-format labels.

## Voice and evidence

Short, lowercase, casual and personalized. Preserve exact URLs, handles, email addresses and identifiers. Use one useful professional observation, not a miniature biography or generic praise. Relevant founders/product/team members need not personally code. Describe historical or client work accurately.

Darwin agent search is the supplied offer. The exact endpoint, working one-line example, free-use conditions and signup destination still require validation. Do not insert untested links or promise execution, exhaustive coverage, unlimited free use or guaranteed results. Offer an example only when it exists.

## COPY 1 EMAIL

Use [email.md](templates/email.md). Usually 20–40 body words, at most 60; subject 1–6 words. When providing two editorial alternatives, neither a nor b has a prescribed structure. One small next step is enough.

## COPY 2 DM

Use [dm.md](templates/dm.md). Usually 15–30 words, at most 40. Keep the opening natural and specific. Do not invent familiarity or an earlier conversation. Channel access and the current operator's capabilities must support the chosen action.

## COPY 3 REPLY TO POST

Use [reply_post.md](templates/reply_post.md). Usually 3–12 words. React to the actual authored post, or ask one relevant question. Omit filler, signatures and requests to DM. For a directly relevant product introduction, up to 30 words and one final verified link are permitted by the voice guide; unsupported channel delivery remains held. Save the exact post URL and author with the private draft.

A reply to an incoming private DM is a separate response: it requires the actual message and must not be mislabeled as a public post reply. Public replies currently remain Markdown drafts until their separate tool storage is implemented.

## Sequence and review

For social → email, record the first social action and email follow-up as separate planned steps. Plan three distinct channels on days 1, 4 and 7 at the earliest, in scored order and at least 72 hours apart if there is no reply. Before a later touch, inspect actual response/history and recipient preferences; do not automatically contact both channels. A/B alternatives are not a sequence.

For contact/location outreach, use the supported relationship or SF evidence and an appropriate meeting question. Never invent an introduction, event attendance or travel plan.

Review exact identity, role context, evidence, route/readiness, natural voice, one clear purpose and factual links before preparing an external action. Save approved voice changes to copy.md and relevant guidance, preserving the exact private revision. The [copy tool guide](README.md) explains current storage capabilities; the [System guide](../../docs/CRM.md) explain channel operation.

## Daily fetched-batch workflow

Follow the selected agent runbook for scope and the saved copy workflow for preparation. Preserve contact limits, actual history and verified outcomes.
