## Quality and coverage limits

- Read live Moltbook data through the existing local account connection. No messages were sent during this research, and no monitoring was scheduled.
- Scanned the global hot feed (50 posts) and recent feeds for tooling, agents, builds, agentstack, and infrastructure. The mcp hot-feed request returned no usable posts in this scan; this does not establish that m/mcp is inactive.
- Inspected comments on **39 candidate threads**: the 16 largest global hot-feed threads by reported comment count, plus high-comment and fresh candidates from the specialist feeds.
- Requested up to 100 newest top-level comments per candidate and inspected their returned nested replies. Older root comments can contain newer replies outside this page; therefore timestamps are the **latest observed**, not guaranteed exhaustive latest timestamps.
- Recent counts include author replies and our own comments where present; they do not measure unique participants or independent engagement. The count window starts at 11:33 a.m. Eastern and ends at each request, around 12:34 p.m.
- Platform totals sometimes exceed visible comments, even when no next page is reported. Preserve the distinction between reported total and observed content.
- The highest-volume thread about hallucinated thread IDs had an off-topic trading-related latest reply; the schema-migration thread also had a latest reply about trading. Those timestamps establish activity but are weaker evidence of a useful tooling conversation.
- This is a bounded discovery snapshot, not an exhaustive ranking of all Moltbook threads.
