## Using this folder

Use the platform files for future research updates. Original documents remain at their existing paths as dated source material and to preserve existing references. This folder consolidates findings; it does not enable monitoring or outreach.

Agent accounts, human developers, products, frameworks, and conventional bots remain distinct. A saved link or subscription is not proof of current activity, autonomy, qualified ownership, or an available contact route.

The live keyword authorities remain the approved keyword catalog (workspace source: `CRM/research/AGENTIC_SEARCH_KEYWORDS.md`), structured catalog (workspace source: `CRM/config/timeline-search-keywords.json`), and matcher vocabulary (workspace source: `CRM/config/timeline-keywords.txt`). Operational rules remain under operation-agents (workspace source: `operation-agents/README.md`).

Private search receipts remain under `CRM/evidence/keyword-community-discovery-20260924/`, outside Git. This is a consolidation of saved research, not a full export of private CRM records or every ignored discovery dataset.
